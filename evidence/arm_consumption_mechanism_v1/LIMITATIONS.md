# Limitations and interpretation boundary

1. The 30 synthetic Python cases had been used before this mechanism replay. They are not an independent held-out confirmatory set.
2. Three seeds repeat the same 30 cases. The inferential unit is the case, not 90 independent samples.
3. One local model was used: `gemma4:26b`, digest `5571076f3d70050487b26b341705799e0ab29b808164f90d20d4cf84f699d251`, Ollama server `0.32.5`, temperature `0`.
4. Semantic scoring was condition-masked blind AI preliminary scoring. Two AI rating passes agreed on 470/540 items; 70 disagreements were adjudicated before unblinding. This is not an independent human raw-packet audit.
5. Internal hashes and locks are present, but no external timestamp, access log, or third-party custody proves that the blind key remained unopened.
6. The freeze did not contain hashes for every post-capture scorer/replay source later used. The historical report therefore records a predeclared `NO-GO` and `publishable=false`.
7. The headline comparison isolates evidence-review enforcement against a length/attention-matched style reviewer on a common draft. It does not isolate SongRyeon's final re-check stage alone and does not establish end-to-end product performance.
8. Public score rows permit exact recounting of archived scores. Independent re-rating is possible from the included condition-masked answer packets and rubric, but any new rating is a new analysis and may disagree with the archived preliminary AI scores.
